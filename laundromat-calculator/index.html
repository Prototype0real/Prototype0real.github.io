Laundromat Profit Calculator

From the video “Why Buying a Laundromat Pays You Almost Nothing”. Defaults are the illustrative store from the video. Not financial advice.

Benchmarks: utilities ≈ 19–21% of revenue, healthy rent 15–25%, staffed payroll ≈ 22% (Coin Laundry Association, American Coin-Op 2026 survey, PlanetLaundry). Equipment cost, equipment life and loan terms are assumptions — use your own.
